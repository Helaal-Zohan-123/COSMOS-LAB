1. Extract the entire ZIP archive into any folder on your computer.
2. Windows: Double-click Launch-COSMOS-LAB.bat (automatically starts a local server on http://127.0.0.1:4173 and opens your browser — works even without Python or Node).
3. Mac / Linux: Double-click Launch-COSMOS-LAB.command (or run ./Launch-COSMOS-LAB.command in Terminal) to start http://127.0.0.1:4173 in your browser.
